# Delivery checklist

- Runnable input and source are present; dependencies and commands are documented.
- Every quantitative claim points to a computed result and recorded version.
- Mesh convergence, equilibrium, reciprocity and negative controls are legible.
- Rod port matrices and bulk C/Q matrices have separate definitions and units.
- Geometry sweeps show material amount and envelope changes.
- Calibration and true holdout validation are not conflated.
- Skills and project can be shared without machine paths, private chats or licenses.
- DOCX figures, formulas and headings have been rendered and checked.
- ZIP delivery is not described as publishing or installing a plugin.
